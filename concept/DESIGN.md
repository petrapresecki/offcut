# Offcut design rules

Learned from how Ohno Type Co, Collletttivo, Uncut, Displaay, 205TF, Koto, basement.studio, teenage engineering and mymind are actually built (fonts, sizes and colours measured with the kit's capture script, October 2026), then applied to Offcut's own idea: the images you couldn't throw away.

## What those sites have in common
- **One family, one weight, small.** 205TF sets everything in one font at 15px. Collletttivo's UI is Ronzino at 11–16px. Uncut uses only Uncut Sans 400 at 18/24/31px. Hierarchy comes from size and grey, almost never from bold.
- **Loud content, quiet interface.** The noise comes from the specimens, the photos or one giant mark (Uncut's wordmark, Collletttivo's cropped letter). Everything around it is plain.
- **Black, white and grey,** plus at most one tiny accent. Ohno and 205TF leave links in the browser's own blue (#0000ee).
- **Almost no motion:** a ticker or a marquee. No fade-ups, no smooth scroll.
- **They name their fonts.**

## Offcut
- **Type:** Ronzino 400/500 for all reading and clicking (15px body, 13px meta in grey, 24/31px for page titles). Sprat (variable, condensed black to extended) only for the one loud thing per screen: the wordmark and big counts. Necto Mono only for colour values. All three by Collletttivo, OFL; credited in the footer.
- **No uppercase tracked micro-labels.** Sentence case, Ronzino, grey.
- **Colour:** paper #f1f0ec and ink #0c0c0c (dark mode: swapped). Links and the main action in plain link blue #0000ee. The images bring every other colour.
- **Shape:** one shape for every control: a grey field with 6px corners (like Uncut's rows). The main action is the same shape in black. No pills, no shadows, no crop marks.
- **The one loud thing:** the cut wordmark. OFFCUT in Sprat, sliced along one diagonal, the lower half slipped along the cut, like a trimmed sheet. On the landing page it sits on a split-fountain field (risograph inks blended on one roller, with real grain) that rolls with the pointer.
- **Motion:** only the cut, and images arriving. Interfaces don't animate in.
- **Captions like an archive:** title, then designer and source in grey, under each image.
