# Offcut — concept research

## Brief
A web app for graphic designers (first user: a graphic-designer friend in Zagreb) to keep the images that inspire them — posters, packaging, branding, type, photos from the street — find them again by colour, and turn them into moodboards for clients. Covers the landing page and the whole app. Petra's feedback on the current design: it feels generic, and the fonts (Archivo wide + IBM Plex Mono) aren't right.

## Subject inventory (things only Offcut has)
1. The name: offcuts are the trimmed scraps from a print shop's guillotine that designers can't throw away.
2. The green self-healing cutting mat every designer works on (grid, centimetre rule, scalpel cuts).
3. The friend's number: 3,723,018 open tabs.
4. Every image arrives with its own palette (the app reads it on save).
5. Search by colour: "everything with this orange".
6. Pantone-style swatch books and fan decks: how designers actually pick colour.
7. Zagreb: the round green advertising columns (oglasni stupovi) layered with pasted posters.
8. Photos taken on a phone in the street (signage, packaging in a shop).
9. The moodboard as a printed proof for a client (PDF, A4/A3, credits page).
10. The client's 👍 / 👎 on a shared board.
11. "Already in your library" — the duplicate check.
12. Print vocabulary: bleed, trim, CMYK, proof, ink traps.

## Argument (landing page, one claim per section)
- You keep too many tabs open → proof: the tab count, closed into one library.
- Every image tells you its colours → proof: the palette read on save, copyable as HEX/RGB/CMYK.
- You can find things by colour → proof: the live colour search on real (drawn) posters.
- References become something you can send → proof: a moodboard and its PDF.

## Current design against creative-sites.md
Fails the defaults audit: mono caps micro-labels everywhere (`.label`), crop marks as decoration (design-tool chrome), "01 · Close your tabs" index numbers, fade-ups and Lenis on every section, an interface hint ("Drag the posters"), several signature effects on one page, wide display type as the default hero.

## Pin walk (what fits)
- lib-rm-dia — the whole archive as a wall of tiny thumbnails: a library is literally this.
- lib-obys-grid / lib-obys-strip — faint vs full-colour, one set of thumbnails, several layouts.
- lib-and2es-pills — one tiny size for all UI, hierarchy from fill only.
- lib-gn-type — a grotesk and a serif paired at the same small size.
- lib-rm-oppen — images hang from one line, ragged bottom, tiny text at the edges.
- lib-horst — one flat colour per section, ink on top, one face at one weight.
- lib-rm-pdfmag — colour bars as structure.
- lib-muller — one bold colour carries the brand, a catalogue ledger.
- lib-an / lib-rm-anochesone — the interaction comes from what the product is (drag).
- lib-rm-wrillustrations — work laid on a table at slightly different sizes.
- lib-k95 / iva-d-spiral / orbit-gallery — images on a turning 3D shape.
- lib-rm-intertapes — real objects are the spectacle, the frame stays plain.
- nope: lib-rm-pangram (template look), iva-n-claude-design (too generic).
